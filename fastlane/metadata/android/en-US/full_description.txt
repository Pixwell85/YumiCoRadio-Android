Yumi Co. Radio is an amateur net radio playing Future Funk, City Pop, Nu Disco and
Vaporwave, around the clock. This is its official Android player, wrapped in a
Windows 9x look — not as a gimmick, but because that is what the station's website
has always been.

Player

Pick your stream: 256 or 128 kbps MP3, or 64 kbps AAC when you are on mobile data.
Playback keeps running in the background and on the lock screen, with notification,
Bluetooth and headset controls. Now playing shows the artist, title, album art,
elapsed time, bitrate, and how many people are listening with you.

The VU meter is real. It is measured from the audio the app is already decoding, so
it moves with the music rather than pretending to — and it needs no microphone
permission to do it.

There is a sleep timer, and a share button for whatever is on the air.

Android Auto

Start the stream and see the current track from the dashboard.

Live chat

The station's own chat, in full: channels, private messages, pixel-art emotes, file
uploads with a daily quota, and presence — online, away or busy. Notifications can be
set to every message, to mentions and private messages only, or to nothing at all.

Reserved nicknames work. The password is asked once per launch and is never written
to disk.

Schedule and history

What is playing now, what is coming up, and the current hour as a live bar. Plus the
recently played list, with artwork.

Appearance

Light and dark, both ported from the website's own themes rather than invented for
the app.

What it does not do

No ads. No accounts. No analytics, no Google Play Services, no Firebase, no tracking
of any kind. The only permissions it asks for are the ones playback and chat need:
network, foreground service, notifications and wake lock.

Free software under the GPLv3. The icons are either public-domain or drawn for this
project, and every asset's licence is documented in the repository.
