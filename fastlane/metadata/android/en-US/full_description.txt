Yumi Co. Radio is a 24/7 net radio playing Future Funk, City Pop, Nu Disco and
Vaporwave. This is the official native Android player, wrapped in an authentic
Windows 98 look.

Tap play and listen anywhere: playback keeps running in the background and on the
lock screen, with full notification and Bluetooth controls. Now-playing shows the
live track, artist, album art and listener count, and a "recently played" list
lets you look back over the last songs. Pick your stream quality (256 or 128 kbps),
set a sleep timer, or share what's on the air.

Built for the car: Yumi Co. Radio supports Android Auto, so you can start the live
stream and see the current track right from your dashboard.

No ads, no accounts, no tracking of any kind. Free and open source (GPLv3).
