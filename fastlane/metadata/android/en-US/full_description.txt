Take Yumi Co. Radio with you wherever you go.

Yumi Co. Radio is a 24/7 online radio station dedicated to Future Funk, City Pop,
Nu Disco and Vaporwave. Its official Android app brings the station to your phone
with a native player and a distinctive retro Windows 9x look.

Listen in the background or from your lock screen, control playback from
notifications, headsets and Bluetooth devices, and follow the current track with
its artwork, artist and title. Browse recently played songs, check the station
schedule, choose your preferred stream quality, use the ten-band equalizer, set a
sleep timer or share what is playing.

Join the live chat to talk with other listeners and the hosts, send private
messages, use emotes, and share images or files.

On the road, Android Auto lets you start the stream and see what is playing
directly from your dashboard.

No ads. No account required. No analytics or tracking. Free and open source under
GPLv3.
